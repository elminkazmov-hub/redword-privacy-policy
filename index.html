Tabu Game — Privacy Policy Last updated: July 29, 2026 Tabu Game ("the app") is an offline party word-guessing game published for Android. This policy explains what information the app and its third-party services handle when you play. What this app is Tabu Game has no user accounts, no login, and no server operated by the developer that stores your personal data. All gameplay happens entirely on your device. Information collected Advertising ID: the app shows ads via Google AdMob, which may access your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads, subject to the consent choices described below and your device's own ad settings. Purchase information: the optional "Ad-Free Forever" one-time purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The developer never receives or stores your payment card details. Locally stored preferences: your language, theme, sound on/off setting, and best-score records are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently. The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, or any other personal data. Third-party services Google AdMob (advertising) — https://policies.google.com/privacy Google Play Billing (purchases) — https://policies.google.com/privacy These services may process data under their own privacy policies, linked above, independent of this app. Your choices If you're located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, a consent form lets you choose how your data is used for ads the first time you open the app. You can change this choice at any time from the app's Settings screen → "Ad Privacy Options" (shown only where applicable). You can also opt out of personalized advertising at any time from your device's own Google settings, independent of this app. Data retention and deletion Since the app keeps no server-side account or profile, uninstalling it removes all locally stored data immediately. There is nothing further to request deletion of. Children's privacy This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. Changes to this policy If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.
